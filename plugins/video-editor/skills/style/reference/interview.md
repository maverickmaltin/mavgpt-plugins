# The style interview

Ask in small batches (two to four questions), each with a suggested answer in brackets, and accept "you pick", "skip" or "not sure". Skip anything the videos will answer better (caption size, cut speed): you'll measure those. Save answers in `style-lab/<style>/interview.md` in their own words, with how sure they were ("maybe" stays "maybe"). Put files they give you in `style-lab/<style>/docs/`.

## Batch 1: what you make

- What kind of videos? Talking to camera / tutorial with screen recordings / vlog / podcast or interview clips / faceless (voiceover over b-roll) / UGC or ads / product demos / comedy or skits / explainers / b-roll montages (travel, food, real estate) / gaming / something else. If several: which one first? Each format can have its own editing style; we can add the others later.
- Where do they go? Instagram Reels / TikTok / YouTube Shorts / YouTube / LinkedIn / X / Facebook / several. [9:16 vertical, 1080x1920]
- How long are they usually? [30 to 60 seconds]
- What language do you speak in them? Ever more than one speaker?

## Batch 2: how you film

- What do you film on? [phone] Front or back camera? Do you know the frame rate (30 or 60)? Is HDR video on? (iPhone HDR footage looks washed out in some editors; we handle it, but it helps to know.)
- Do your raw videos come trimmed already (you cut them in the camera app, Instagram Edits, CapCut...) or straight from the camera?
- Do you add text in the app before sending it to an editor (TikTok-style text boxes, stickers)? If yes, the edit has to keep and work around them.
- Any screen recordings, b-roll or photos that usually go in?

## Batch 3: your brand and documents

Ask them to drop in anything they have. Each one saves a lot of guessing:
- A brand book or brand guidelines (colors, fonts, logo rules).
- An editing SOP, an editor brief, a checklist, or notes they've sent an editor (these are gold: they're the rules).
- Font files (.ttf or .otf), logo (a PNG with a transparent background), color codes.
- A LUT or color preset (.cube), or the filter they always use.
- Their sound effect pack, music they've licensed, an intro or outro clip, a lower third or end card.
- Editing-app templates (CapCut, Premiere, Final Cut, DaVinci): you can't open those directly, so ask for an exported example video or screenshots instead.

## Batch 4: taste

- Three words for how your videos should feel. [clear, fast, friendly]
- What do you hate seeing in edits? (too many zooms, emojis, music, flashy transitions, captions covering the face, one-word captions...) These become "never" rules.
- What must always be there? (a hook title, captions on every word, your logo, a call to action at the end, your handle...)
- Music: none / trending audio you add in the app after / music under the voice / only at the start or end. Do you have music you're allowed to use?
- Sound effects: none / a few subtle ones / lots.
- When you mention something (an app, a product, a number, a place), should it appear on screen? Who provides it: you (screenshots, screen recordings, b-roll), the AI finds it (official images, websites), or the AI makes it (graphics, recreated app screens, AI-generated clips)? Anything that must never be faked or recreated?
- Route B or C: what do you like about each reference creator, and is there anything of theirs you DON'T want?
- Route A: which of your videos are edited exactly how you like, and which aren't? Did a human editor make them? Has your style changed lately (then only the recent ones count)?

## Batch 5: how you want to work

- Finished videos ready to post, or a clean rough cut you finish in your editing app (CapCut, Premiere, Final Cut, DaVinci)? A rough cut comes with a captions file (.srt) and the storyboard notes.
- Should I show you a storyboard before every edit, or only when the video is unusual? [every time for the first few]
- Can I suggest the hook title and on-screen text, or always use your exact words?
- File names and versions: anything you always need (two hooks, a 16:9 version, a 30-second cut)? Where do finished videos go? [exports/]
- Is anyone else reviewing (a client, a manager)? Their notes can go into the style too.

## After the interview

Read the answers back in five or six lines and fix anything they correct. Then go to the videos (`getting-videos.md`).
